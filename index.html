OhanaLedger

Household money and family health records, kept together on your phone.

Get it on Google Play

For Android phones and tablets

The household’s money

  • Every account in one place

    Cash, bank, e-wallets like GCash and Maya, credit cards and investments, with what came in and what went out.

  • Bills and paydays

    Recurring bills with a reminder before each is due, the paydays you expect, and a 45-day forecast.

  • Saving for something

    Set a goal and see how close you are.

  • Where the money went

    Spending by category and by month, and shared costs split between members.

The family’s health

  • A record for each person

    Visits, allergies, conditions, immunisations and measurements such as weight and blood pressure.

  • Medication and doses

    What to take and when, with a reminder for each dose.

  • Pregnancy and growth

    Weeks counted from the due date, and children’s growth charted against WHO standards.

  • An emergency card

    Allergies, medication and who to call, readable without unlocking the app.

Your records stay on your phone

  • There is no account and no OhanaLedger server. We never receive what you record.
  • Backups are files you make yourself, encrypted on your phone, and saved wherever you choose.
  • Share on local network lets a laptop on your own Wi-Fi open the app’s screens, guarded by a PIN, and stops when you tap Stop.
  • Ads from Google AdMob appear on a few money and overview pages. They never appear on health pages, and your records are never given to Google.
